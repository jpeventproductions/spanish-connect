Practice notes +

The connecting-word set uses sentences with entonces, luego, dónde, donde, cuándo, cuando, porque, pero, antes and después. Important words are highlighted.

“-tion” often corresponds to “-ción,” with exceptions. Spanish often omits subject pronouns when the verb and context make the meaning clear. These 300 phrases are practical examples, not a frequency-ranked list.

Spanish word pace counts the words in correctly matched phrases, including repeated words. A phrase with a mistake stays in the retry bucket until you match it without a mistake in a later round.
